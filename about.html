Our story
& mission.

Zeus Bus started with a simple observation: getting to campus safely and on time shouldn't be the hardest part of a student's day.

Who we are

A student-built answer to a student problem.

Zeus Bus is Assiut's student-focused transportation network. We recognized the daily struggle university students face trying to secure safe, dependable transport to campus — and built a service around solving exactly that.

We don't just run trips. We turn the commute into a stress-free launchpad for the rest of your academic day — organized pickups, consistent timing, and a calm, air-conditioned ride.

Our Vision

Egypt's smartest micro-transit, built for campus life.

Every decision — routes, timing, comfort — is engineered around the rhythm of the academic day.

Punctuality

Schedules mapped directly to university lecture timings.

Safety protocols

Verified, professional drivers holding controlled, monitored speeds.

Student atmosphere

A quiet, air-conditioned environment built for reading and resting.

Constant scale

New pickup points added quickly based on real student demand.

Why "Zeus"

Strength and operational leadership.

The name represents the standard we hold ourselves to. Our baseline promise stays simple: better vibe, better ride. We combine careful scheduling with modern vehicle standards so you never drop a lecture hour to a late bus.
