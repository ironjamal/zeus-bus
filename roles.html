Community Standards

Our roles
& respect.

A shared set of standards that keep every ride calm, safe, and on time — for everyone on board.

Respect the space

Keep the bus clean. Use trash bins and avoid leaving personal items behind so it's comfortable for the next rider too.

Volume control

Maintain a moderate noise level. Use headphones for music or video to respect anyone studying or resting.

Safety first

Remain seated while the bus is in motion. Avoid distracting the driver and follow all boarding instructions.

Punctuality

Be at your designated stop 5 minutes early. Respecting the schedule helps everyone get to class on time.

The Zeus Goal

By following these roles, we create an environment that supports every student's academic journey.

On board and on time.
